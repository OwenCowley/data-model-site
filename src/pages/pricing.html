Questions

Can I leave and take my models with me?+

Yes. Models are plain YAML/JSON files in your own Git repository on every plan. Portability is never a paid feature.

Do I need an AI provider or a cloud account?+

No. The core runs fully offline. AI context is exported as files your own tools can read.

Which platforms are supported?+

The first target is Databricks, dbt and Atlan. Today that works from exported metadata files; live read-only connectors are in development with design partners.

How do design partners pay?+

Design partners get early access on preferential terms in exchange for regular feedback. Apply here.
